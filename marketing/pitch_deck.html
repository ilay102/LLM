How we prove quality

Factual vs. stylistic — separated, audited.

Every prompt is sent both through VIREN and direct to your baseline. Three independent LLM judges from two model families (Sonnet, GPT-4o, Opus) compare the answers in randomized A/B order. Then we split every disagreement into two buckets:

  • Factually wrong / incomplete — the only ones that actually matter. 10%.
  • Stylistic-only — both correct, just different in tone or length. 3%.

Every regression is auditable in the per-prompt report. You see what was wrong and why. We don't claim "80% W-T" — we claim 90% factually equivalent or better, and prove it.
